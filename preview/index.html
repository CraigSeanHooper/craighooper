Working on design systems and digital modernization for Canadian Blood Services

I still love it. I love to solve problems, and I enjoy distilling the complex into something simpler. We encounter problematic digital experiences every day, and I enjoy playing my part in shaping a better way. I'm a big believer in systems, and in how a well-crafted one keeps design consistent.

The rapid rise of agentic workflows fills me with a mix of amazement, curiosity, and caution.
